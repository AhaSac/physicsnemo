In our experience managing CFD workloads in an industrial                     ROM, DMD) remain highly competitive on accuracy and
speed, and their interpretability is an operational advantage
where engineers must justify results. Neural surrogates’ value
lies elsewhere — geometry generalization, high-dimensional
parameter spaces, differentiability for optimization — not
raw accuracy on every problem. The entire pipeline runs
on a single consumer GPU workstation with open-source
tools, suggesting this kind of practitioner-level sanity check
is broadly replicable without institutional compute access, and
may be a useful complement to large-scale HPC benchmarking
efforts within this community.
