    With the recent advances of deep neural networks in
various applications, including computer vision and natural
language processing, [17] applying neural networks to scien-
tific problems has been investigated. For example, Alphafold
[18] predicts protein structures successfully, and FourCastNet
[19] forecasts weather and the traditional numerical weather
simulation. However, the expressive power of neural networks
is limited by data size, it is only easily applicable to some
scientific problems where data acquisition is not highly limited
or expensive. A physics-informed neural network [20] is de-
signed to address such problems by incorporating prior knowl-
